4. Model Strengths, Limitations & Improvements

5 / 5 Marks

✔ Core Strengths

  • Zero Data Leakage: Encapsulated inside Scikit-Learn Pipeline; all imputers and scalers fit on training folds only.
  • High Clinical Precision: 95.65% precision prevents damaging false reassurance for vulnerable students.
  • Probability Calibration: Out-of-fold Platt scaling ensures output numbers represent empirical probabilities.
  • Explainable Decisions: XGBoost surrogate engine produces exact TreeSHAP waterfall and beeswarm plots.
  • Salary Prediction: Auxiliary XGBRegressor estimates CTC packages in INR with uncertainty intervals.

⚠ Identified Limitations

  • Corpus Size (N=215): Benchmark dataset has 215 student rows; expanding to multi-institutional cohorts will increase statistical power.
  • Synthetic Master's Pathway: Postgraduate fields (has_masters, masters_type) were synthetically augmented to model multi-degree pathways.
  • Missing Soft-Skills Data: Historical tables lack qualitative interview metrics such as spoken English fluency and body language.

★ Future Improvements

  • Multimodal Interview AI: Integrate speech-to-text NLP and facial video analysis for automated behavioral scoring.
  • Longitudinal Recurrent Models: Track semester-by-semester GPA trends using LSTM/GRU networks to identify downward trajectories early.
  • Federated University Learning: Train collaboratively across multiple college databases while preserving student data privacy.

8. Final Presentation & Individual Viva Voce

8 / 8 Marks

Review these examiner questions and structured answers to guarantee full marks during your viva:

Q1: Why did Support Vector Classifier (RBF-SVC) outperform tree ensembles like XGBoost and Random Forest? ▾
On tabular datasets with small-to-medium sample sizes (N=215), deep decision trees create fragmented partitions across sparse one-hot encoded categories, resulting in higher variance. In contrast, RBF-SVC projects the feature space into a continuous Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space and maximizes the geometric classification margin. This produced lower cross-validation variance (σ = 0.0385) and the highest mean CV ROC-AUC (0.9417).
Q2: How did you guarantee zero data leakage in your preprocessing pipeline? ▾
We encapsulated all data preprocessing inside a Scikit-Learn ColumnTransformer. Imputation medians, standard scaling means/variances, and SMOTE oversampling are fitted strictly on the inner training folds during cross-validation, and never on the validation or held-out test splits.
Q3: Why did you tune the decision threshold from 0.50 to 0.745? ▾
In campus recruitment, prediction errors carry asymmetric costs. A False Positive (wrongly assuring an at-risk student that they are safe) leads to complacency and recruitment failure. In contrast, a False Negative (advising an eligible student to attend upskilling) causes minimal harm. Shifting τ to 0.745 maximized the validation F1-score and drove precision on the held-out test set to 95.65% with only 1 false positive.
Q4: Why was Platt Sigmoid Calibration chosen over Isotonic Regression? ▾
Isotonic Regression is non-parametric and overfits on smaller validation samples, causing its Brier loss to degrade to 0.1347. Parametric Platt Sigmoid calibration generalized smoothly, lowering validation Brier score to 0.1212 and test Brier score to 0.1159, ensuring probabilities align with actual empirical placement rates.
Q5: How did you explain an RBF-SVC model that has no native tree-split feature importances? ▾
Because RBF-SVC operates in a dual Hilbert space without linear weights or tree splits, we implemented an Interpretable Surrogate Architecture: an XGBoost model (CV ROC-AUC: 0.9264) was trained to distill the champion model's predictions, enabling exact TreeSHAP computation for global beeswarm plots and instance-level counterfactual guidance.
Q6: How does the system detect and manage data drift in production? ▾
PlaceIQ incorporates an automated Population Stability Index (PSI) monitoring module (src/drift.py). It quantifies distribution divergence between training baseline features and incoming inference batches. A PSI < 0.10 indicates stability, while PSI ≥ 0.25 triggers an automated retraining alert.
